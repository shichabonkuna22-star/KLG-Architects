Strandloper Boutique Hotel

DATE OF COMPLETION: 2016- LOCATION: Paternoster CLIENT: Strandloper Boutique Hotel PHOTOGRAPHY / IMAGERY CREDIT: Strandloper & KLG ARCHITECTS

Situated in the coastal village of Paternoster, the unique cottages project by KLG architects is a unique development under the Strandloper hotel Group brand. The layout aims to enhance the distinctive character, scale and authenticity of the fishing village - providing a unique destination right outside of Cape Town. Buildings draw from the local vernacular and focus on a contemporary simplified design solutions.

The precinct programme includes; Hotel, administration block, spa retreat, wellness suites, gym, and a heritage walkway route.
