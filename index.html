Vernacular building traditions, contemporary form.

KLG Architects was founded out of the Köper Levin George architectural practice, having originally been started by Glennie McIntosh and John Egan in Cape Town in the 1930s, and joined by Henk Sikkel in the 1940s (then named Glennie, Egan and Sikkel). The studio works from 1 Hans Ras Road, Rondebosch.
