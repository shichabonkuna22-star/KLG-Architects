Pride in the built environment.

The name IQHAYIYA translates to pride in isiXhosa. It embodies the ethos to create built environment spaces and places that end-users and communities can find pride in and ownership of.

A design-orientated firm serving the public sector.

iQhayiya Design Workshop (PTY) Ltd. is a professional architectural firm established and led by Kayalethu Qwalela and Mfundo Maphumulo. Both directors are registered as Professional Architects with the SACAP. The studio works from 88 Marine Drive, Margate, KwaZulu-Natal.
